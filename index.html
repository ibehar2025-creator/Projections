Model Notes

This is a personal research tool, not financial advice. The projection model follows a long-term valuation workflow: estimate future revenue, convert it to earnings with net margin, adjust share count for dilution or buybacks, then apply an exit multiple.

The private 1000x Stocks calculator formula is not publicly documented, so this app uses transparent assumptions that you can edit.
